## Cart

**What happens on "Add to Cart"** (`data-testid="addtocart_btn"`):

1. **The upsell modal "Upgrade to Certified Organic" opens first** (`role=dialog`). It offers "Organic Mattress Upgrade +$100", with **No Thanks** / **Upgrade Now** / ✕. Nothing is added yet.
2. After **No Thanks**, the button shows a loading (shimmer) state. Then:
   - `POST https://api.qa-env.residenthome.com/carts/my/items?lang=en&brand=awara` → **200**
     - request: `{"items":[{"quantity":1,"productID":"695e90e002e98063df50e116","sku":"awnluxhym-s:queen","properties":[{"name":"Size","value":"queen",…}]}]}`
     - response: `{ status:"ok", result:{ id, itemsCount:1, totalPrice:{amount:69900,currency:"$"}, items:[{ sku:"awnluxhym-s:queen", quantity:1, … }], … } }`
   - `GET /carts/my?lang=en&brand=awara` → 200, then `GET /carts/{id}/remoteToken` → 200
3. **Client-side redirect to `/checkout/cart`.** There's no drawer and no toast. (An empty MUI right-anchored Drawer is mounted but has no content.)
4. The header cart icon (testid `cart-icon`) shows **`1`**. That's visible on the PDP; the checkout header on `/checkout/cart` has no cart icon.
5. **A second upsell modal appears on the cart page**: "awara + Extend", a 10-year protection plan for $79, with **No Thanks** / **Add Protection** / ✕.

## Flakiness risks

1. **Delayed overlays**: the cookie banner (seconds) and the promo email modal (~16 s) appear at unpredictable times and take focus or cover controls. Register `page.addLocatorHandler()` for both, and/or pre-seed consent cookies.
2. **The upsell modal blocks add-to-cart**: the item is only added after "No Thanks". A test that clicks "Add to Cart" and goes straight to the cart will fail. The modal is probably driven by Dynamic Yield (`/api/dynamic-yield/flags`), so it may not show for every visitor (A/B).
3. **A/B testing / personalization**: Dynamic Yield (`_dy*` cookies, `/api/dynamic-yield/selectors`), a `release_candidate` cookie, and promo copy that varies (Summer vs Memorial Day). Avoid asserting marketing text.
4. **Client-side routing**: route changes don't fire `load`. Use `toHaveURL` and element assertions, not navigation waits.
5. **Async price/quote loading**: the PDP calls `carts/quote` and `applicable-offers` after render, and prices and the Affirm "As low as" line fill in late (shimmer placeholders). Assert prices with auto-retrying `expect`.
6. **Countdown timers**: on the hero, the PDP promo banner and the cart reservation (≈10 min). Their text changes every second, so never assert on it. The cart reservation may expire in slow CI runs.
7. **Duplicate responsive DOM**: hidden mobile copies share testids (`hero_shop_mattress_btn` ×3, `cart-icon` ×2, `mobile_logo`). Testid-only locators hit strict-mode violations.
8. **Sticky footer "Add To Cart"** appears after scrolling (`mattress_sticky_footer_section_btn`). It's another match for loose locators.
9. **Bot protection**: `datadome` and `isBot` cookies are set. Heavy parallel CI runs could get challenged. (The API itself, `api.qa-env.residenthome.com`, returned a Cloudflare 403 to non-browser clients at the time; resolved with the `E2EUI-Tests-Brands-CI` User-Agent, see #24.)
10. **Shared server-side cart**: the cart persists through cookies. Reusing `storageState` across tests would leak items between tests.
11. **Third-party noise**: Intercom, UserWay, QuestionPro, Attentive, Quantum Metric, Google Ads. The console showed ~10–27 errors per page (400 on `/metrics/...` Google Ads beacons, CORS errors from Amazon Pay, 404 images). Don't fail tests on console errors.
12. **Header: hover-driven mega menus and a hidden duplicate nav.** Menus open on hover, so a test that moves the mouse over the header (e.g. on the way to the cart icon) can open a menu that covers content. The inert hidden `nav "Navigation"` duplicates every header link, so role/text locators resolve to it first.
13. **Announcement bar auto-rotates** and its arrows respond inconsistently. Never assert the visible slide right after clicking an arrow.
